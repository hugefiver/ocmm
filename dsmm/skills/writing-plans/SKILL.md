# Writing Plans

Write an outcome-oriented plan that enables a capable implementer to act without rediscovering material decisions.

- State approved goal, desired end state, scope exclusions and useful acceptance evidence.
- Describe current state, interfaces, dependencies, ownership boundaries, ordering, risks and safeguards. Group independent outcomes into waves; include paths and commands when they prevent ambiguity.
- Complex business or behavior implementation defaults to planner → plan-critic → implementation. A bounded, simple low-risk change may skip; having multiple steps alone does not force a plan.
- Give the critic real plan and evidence. Address substantive blockers; do not require receipt hashes, fixed revision rounds or repeated review after editorial changes.
- Planning does not authorize Git writes or broaden permissions. Escalate decisions changing scope, safety/data, public APIs or irreversible actions.
